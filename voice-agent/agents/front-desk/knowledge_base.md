## About BongoHive
- BongoHive is Zambia's leading innovation hub, founded in 2011. Its tagline is Technology, Entrepreneurship, Innovation.
- It has supported over 200 startups, and its alumni have raised over one million US dollars.
- Services: technology consulting, innovation consulting, coworking and workspace, Data and AI through the BongoHive AI Lab, and entrepreneurship support with mentorship and investor access.

## AI Lab
- The BongoHive AI Lab helps businesses, governments and communities in Africa adopt AI, and builds local AI skills.
- It offers a free AI assessment to find where AI fits in a business, training sessions, SaaS tools, enterprise AI, and support from strategy to adoption.
- Its research is free: State of AI in Zambia 2024, and a mapping of AI in local Zambian languages.
- Training sessions can be booked on the AI Lab website. Pricing: TBC.

- Private Office: 24/7 access, a fully dedicated space, and eight meeting-room hours a month.
- Virtual Office: a business address, mail handling, a dedicated phone line and on-demand meeting rooms.

## Frequently asked questions
- Q: Does BongoHive work outside Zambia? A: Yes, mostly remotely, with in-person meetings arranged as needed.
- Q: Can BongoHive help with prototyping? A: Yes, mainly digital prototypes such as clickable ones, and it trains teams to build their own.
